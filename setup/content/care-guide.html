Bidriware needs very little care. Treat it gently and the black stays deep and the silver stays bright for generations.

  • Dust with a soft, dry cloth.
  • Once a month, rub on a little coconut oil with a soft cloth to keep the black rich, then wipe off the excess.
  • Keep away from water, soap and metal polish. Polish strips the black finish.
  • Display it indoors, away from damp.
  • Pieces with a steel inner chamber (like the hip flask) can be rinsed inside with warm water. Dry the outside straight away.
