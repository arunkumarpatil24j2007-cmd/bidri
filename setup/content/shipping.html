Every order ships free, fully insured, anywhere in India.

Where we ship

We currently deliver within India only. If you would like a piece sent abroad, message us and we will see what is possible.

How long it takes

Orders are delivered by courier in 3–5 business days after dispatch. Remote pin codes can take a little longer. As soon as your parcel is dispatched we email you the courier name and a tracking link.

Cost

Shipping is free on every order. Prices on the site include all taxes.

Packing & insurance

Each piece is wrapped and boxed to travel safely and is insured in transit. Your GI Tag No. 12 authenticity certificate travels in the same box.

If something arrives damaged

Tell us within 7 days of delivery and we will replace it. See Returns & replacements.
