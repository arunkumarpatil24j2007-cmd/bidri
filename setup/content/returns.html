Each piece is made and inspected by hand. If yours is damaged on the way to you, we replace it.

7-day transit guarantee

If your order arrives damaged, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a few photos of the piece and the packaging. We will arrange a free pickup and send a replacement. If an identical replacement is not available, we will refund you in full instead.

Tip: please record a short video while opening the parcel. It makes any claim with the courier quick and simple.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order any time before it is dispatched by messaging us with your order number. We refund the full amount.

Refunds

Approved refunds are made to your original payment method (UPI, card or netbanking) through Razorpay. They usually reach your account within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

Handmade variations

Bidriware is engraved and inlaid freehand, so small differences in pattern, size and finish from the photos are the mark of the hand, not a defect.
