Bidriware is protected by a Geographical Indication — GI Tag No. 12.

A Geographical Indication means only work made in the Bidar region, in the traditional way, can be sold as Bidriware. It protects the artisans who keep the craft alive, and it protects you from imitations.

What makes it genuine

  • A zinc-copper alloy body, cast and finished by hand.
  • Designs engraved freehand, without stencils.
  • Inlay of 99.9% pure silver wire or sheet — never synthetic foil.
  • Blackened with a paste of soil from near Bidar Fort.

Your certificate

Every piece we sell ships with its GI Tag No. 12 authenticity certificate in the box.
