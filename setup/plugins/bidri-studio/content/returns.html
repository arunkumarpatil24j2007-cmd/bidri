Each piece is made and inspected by hand. We don’t take returns or cancellations, but if something isn’t right, we exchange it.

Exchanges

Ask within 7 days of delivery on our Request an exchange page with your order number and a few photos of the piece and the packaging. We reply within 2 working days.

  • Damaged in transit, wrong piece or a fault in the making: we arrange a free pickup and send you the same piece, or another of the same value if it is no longer available.
  • You’d like a different piece: the piece must be unused and in its original packaging with the GI certificate. You pay the courier both ways, and any difference in price.
  • If no exchange suits you, we can give you store credit for the full value instead, valid for 12 months.

Tip: please record a short video while opening the parcel. It makes any damage claim quick and simple.

No cancellations or returns

Each order is prepared for you as soon as it is placed, so orders can’t be cancelled once paid, and pieces can’t be returned for a refund. If we can’t fulfil your order (for example, a piece sold out at the same moment), we refund you in full to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Handmade variations

Bidriware is engraved and inlaid freehand, so small differences in pattern, size and finish from the photos are the mark of the hand, not a defect.
