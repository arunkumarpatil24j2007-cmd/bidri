This policy explains what personal information Bidri Crafts collects when you use this website, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, delivery and billing address, phone number, email address and any note you add (for example a gift message).
  • If you create an account: your login details and order history.
  • When you browse: cookies that keep your bag and login working. We do not use advertising cookies.

We never see or store your full card, UPI or bank details. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay.

Why we use it

To process and deliver your order, send you order and shipping updates, answer your questions, and meet our legal and tax obligations.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay — to process your payment.
  • Shiprocket and its courier partners — your name, address and phone number, to deliver your order.
  • Our web host and email provider — to run this website and send order emails.

We do not sell your information or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see, correct or erase your personal data, or withdraw consent. Contact us and we will respond promptly.
